# ING-06 — Structured repertory and materia medica research

**Status:** In progress. The first schema and manual review API slice is implemented; research integration and end-to-end acceptance remain open. Implement before broader ING-03/04/05 collection and format expansion.

**Implementation checkpoint — 2026-10-10:** Migration 040 adds revision-scoped materia medica/rubric entries, rubric associations and cross-references, a remedy/alias dictionary, exact page/block support, review decisions and publication/index-gated eligibility views. Admin APIs can create remedies and draft entries/associations, then accept or reject reviewed mappings; reviewer APIs list the current entries and associations. Exact Unicode text matching, source/revision foreign keys, required grade schemes and NULL unknown grades are enforced in PostgreSQL. A clean disposable migration and synthetic constraint fixtures passed. No extraction adapter, automatic alias review, structured retrieval/answer route, original navigation UI, correction-to-new-revision flow or baseline evaluation is claimed by this checkpoint. The existing research route still declines structured repertory questions.

**User outcome:** A researcher can ask about a remedy description, look up a repertory rubric, or compare literature categories, see source-specific evidence and limitations, and open each exact original passage or entry.

**PRD basis:** [Product roadmap](../Homeopathy_AI_Product_Requirements_and_Roadmap.md) sections 40 and 42; [POC PRD](../Homeopathy_AI_Source_Ingestion_POC_PRD.md) section 64. Depends on [ING-02](ING-02.md) classification, filtering, saved assets and non-PDF citations. Existing PDF sources must participate; do not create a separate ungoverned repertory corpus.

## Complete feature slice

1. **Reviewed domain records.** Add revision-scoped materia medica entries, source-scoped hierarchical rubrics, rubric/remedy associations, cross-references and a shared remedy/alias dictionary. Each assertion retains source spelling, edition, source/asset/revision and supporting original location(s). Preserve unresolved aliases and conflicting editions; do not merge preparations by name alone. Keep categories separate from evidence type, format and structure-readiness status. Justify schema additions against the shared document model.
2. **Extraction and review.** Implement adapters against inspected, permitted representative materia medica and repertory layouts. Preserve remedy entry/subsection boundaries across pages and rubric ancestry across sections. Retain original typography/notation and the source's grade scheme. Reviewers can accept, correct or reject mapped entries, aliases and grades against an inert original view. Unknown grades remain null/unknown, not zero; do not invent membership or grade from a flattened passage. Corrections create a new processing revision and invalidate affected structured approvals/index candidates while preserving published historical evidence. Unsupported layouts have explicit limitations; one adapter is not universal repertory support.
3. **Category-aware RAG integration.** Add verified structured rubric/remedy lookup alongside lexical/vector passage retrieval in both Quick and Deep. Remedy-description questions prioritise materia medica; rubric questions use verified rubric records; mixed questions combine eligible evidence with category labels. Other categories remain searchable through their reviewed passage structure. Implement roadmap section 42.3 chunk boundaries, contextual index metadata, reviewed alias expansion, hybrid/structured candidate retrieval, relevance reranking and bounded evidence selection across requested sources/categories. Preserve negation, modalities and rubric ancestry. Category fit is a relevance signal, not an implicit exclusion in All literature searches. Deduplicate without merging editions; validate membership/grade claims against structured evidence. Feed accepted automatic/admin classifications into index metadata and routing; classification corrections refresh affected indexes without mixing stale revisions or rewriting saved answers. Persist candidate scores and reranking decisions; re-embed only when embedding input changes. All routes and generated subqueries honor intersecting source/category filters, access, publication and compatible-index gates. Save routes, scope, source/revision IDs, structured record references and coverage limitations in the research trail/evaluation report.
4. **Answers and original reader.** Show full rubric path, original remedy notation and known source grade for repertory evidence; show remedy and subsection for materia medica. Explain source-specific grading where used without treating it as clinical efficacy or model confidence. Attribute descriptions, repertory listings and research findings separately; preserve disagreements. Each claim opens the exact saved supporting entry/passage, including all needed supporting locations where context spans pages. Cite eligible text with limitations when structured readiness is absent; decline unsupported grade/membership/completeness claims. Saved answers resolve without a live fetch or another model call.
5. **Lifecycle and coverage.** Reuse durable jobs and existing review/publication/index infrastructure. Restart, cancellation, retry and reprocessing must not duplicate structured records or embeddings. Changed-source revisions preserve historical citations and do not silently overwrite grade interpretations. Record adapter/version and validation status. Text readiness, structured readiness and collection coverage are separate visible states. Disable/restore and current access restrictions apply to both passage and structured evidence.

## Acceptance

- Compare category-aware RAG with the existing generic passage baseline on the same reviewed questions, corpus and model. Record relevant-evidence recall, claim/citation correctness and filter isolation by category, including unsupported questions; report regressions and measured gains rather than assuming improvement. Include a misleading automatic category corrected by an admin, proof that All literature does not hide relevant evidence due to a category guess, and correct refreshed routing with unchanged historical citations.

- A reviewed materia medica entry spanning a page/section boundary answers a remedy-description question with correct heading context and exact original citations; adjacent remedies do not contaminate it.
- A reviewed repertory fixture preserves nested rubrics, cross-references, original abbreviations and known grades. Missing typography, an ambiguous alias and a different edition's grade scheme produce unresolved states rather than guesses. A rubric question uses only verified associations and identifies the selected edition.
- Quick and Deep answer a mixed materia medica/repertory comparison and a question from at least one other category, such as philosophy or a research article. Evidence labels and attribution remain distinct; no classical or repertory claim becomes a clinical-efficacy claim.
- A mixed work uses section categories correctly. Source/category intersections, empty selections/results and unavailable structure never silently broaden retrieval. Reference-only, denied, disabled, unpublished and incompatible sources cannot enter any retrieval route.
- Review corrections and candidate replacement preserve old answer/category snapshots and original citations. Job retry/restart/cancellation preserve unique records and coverage. Collection-specific pagination/limits checks remain part of ING-03; XML-specific checks remain part of ING-04.
- Focused parser, hierarchy/grade, alias, retrieval, authorization and citation checks plus relevant Go tests and frontend build pass. Exercise source review → publication/index → Quick/Deep → saved original navigation with permitted representative material. Record the assets/revisions, supported adapter layouts and unresolved limitations. Record live checks separately from fixtures; do not claim support for a named website without exercising it.

## Boundaries and following work

No full patient repertorization, automated remedy ranking/prescribing, diagnosis or exhaustive literature coverage is implied. No automatic clinical-evidence upgrade follows from category or grade. ING-03 adds bounded linked collections; ING-04 adds schema-aware XML; ING-05 extends the same classification, structured-readiness, retrieval and citation contracts across those formats/collections. PROD-01 operational recovery remains deferred per the product priority.
