# ING-06 — Structured repertory, materia medica and historical casebook research

**Status:** In progress. The first schema and manual review API slice is implemented; research integration and end-to-end acceptance remain open. Implement before broader ING-03/04/05 collection and format expansion.

**Implementation checkpoint — 2026-10-10:** Migration 040 adds revision-scoped materia medica/rubric entries, rubric associations and cross-references, a remedy/alias dictionary, exact page/block support, review decisions and publication/index-gated eligibility views. Admin APIs can create remedies and draft entries/associations, then accept or reject reviewed mappings; reviewer APIs list the current entries and associations. Exact Unicode text matching, source/revision foreign keys, required grade schemes and NULL unknown grades are enforced in PostgreSQL. A clean disposable migration and synthetic constraint fixtures passed. No extraction adapter, automatic alias review, structured retrieval/answer route, original navigation UI, correction-to-new-revision flow or baseline evaluation is claimed by this checkpoint. The existing research route still declines structured repertory questions.

**User outcome:** A researcher can ask about a remedy description, look up a repertory rubric, inspect an attributed historical case and treatment sequence, or compare literature categories, see source-specific evidence and limitations, and open each exact original passage or entry.

**Linked-document requirement — 2026-10-10 (not yet implemented):** An admin can import a selected linked book or document collection, review its coverage and extracted substantive content, and publish only meaningful, approved evidence. The Homeoint Kent links are examples of index → section → content/anchor navigation, not a restriction to Generalities, Kent, or one website. Discovery is reusable across collections; semantic extraction uses explicit format/layout adapters.

**Linked-discovery checkpoint — 2026-10-10:** A bounded, admin-only collection preview now accepts explicit hosts/path prefixes and document/depth/byte/time limits. The shared safe fetcher checks collection scope before the initial request and every redirect. The preview resolves relative and sibling links against final URLs, separates fragment-free fetch identity from anchors, reports cycles/duplicates, missing anchors, failures and limit exhaustion, and labels index-only versus candidate-content pages. The Add source screen displays the manifest without creating a source. Fixture tests cover sibling paths, multiple anchors, cycles, out-of-scope links, failures, limits and redirect escape. Durable collection capture, coverage review, content filtering, structured adapters and research integration remain open; no named live website has passed application end-to-end acceptance. The separate 2026-10-10 inspection below verifies public linked-page structure only.

## Complete feature slice

1. **Reviewed domain records.** Add revision-scoped materia medica entries, source-scoped hierarchical rubrics, rubric/remedy associations, cross-references and a shared remedy/alias dictionary. Each assertion retains source spelling, edition, source/asset/revision and supporting original location(s). Preserve unresolved aliases and conflicting editions; do not merge preparations by name alone. Keep categories separate from evidence type, format and structure-readiness status. Justify schema additions against the shared document model.
2. **Extraction and review.** Implement adapters against inspected, permitted representative materia medica and repertory layouts. Preserve remedy entry/subsection boundaries across pages and rubric ancestry across sections. Retain original typography/notation and the source's grade scheme. Reviewers can accept, correct or reject mapped entries, aliases and grades against an inert original view. Unknown grades remain null/unknown, not zero; do not invent membership or grade from a flattened passage. Corrections create a new processing revision and invalidate affected structured approvals/index candidates while preserving published historical evidence. Unsupported layouts have explicit limitations; one adapter is not universal repertory support.
3. **Category-aware RAG integration.** Add verified structured rubric/remedy lookup alongside lexical/vector passage retrieval in both Quick and Deep. Remedy-description questions prioritise materia medica; rubric questions use verified rubric records; mixed questions combine eligible evidence with category labels. Other categories remain searchable through their reviewed passage structure. Implement roadmap section 42.3 chunk boundaries, contextual index metadata, reviewed alias expansion, hybrid/structured candidate retrieval, relevance reranking and bounded evidence selection across requested sources/categories. Preserve negation, modalities and rubric ancestry. Category fit is a relevance signal, not an implicit exclusion in All literature searches. Deduplicate without merging editions; validate membership/grade claims against structured evidence. Feed accepted automatic/admin classifications into index metadata and routing; classification corrections refresh affected indexes without mixing stale revisions or rewriting saved answers. Persist candidate scores and reranking decisions; re-embed only when embedding input changes. All routes and generated subqueries honor intersecting source/category filters, access, publication and compatible-index gates. Save routes, scope, source/revision IDs, structured record references and coverage limitations in the research trail/evaluation report.
4. **Answers and original reader.** Show full rubric path, original remedy notation and known source grade for repertory evidence; show remedy and subsection for materia medica. Explain source-specific grading where used without treating it as clinical efficacy or model confidence. Attribute descriptions, repertory listings and research findings separately; preserve disagreements. Each claim opens the exact saved supporting entry/passage, including all needed supporting locations where context spans pages. Cite eligible text with limitations when structured readiness is absent; decline unsupported grade/membership/completeness claims. Saved answers resolve without a live fetch or another model call.
5. **Lifecycle and coverage.** Reuse durable jobs and existing review/publication/index infrastructure. Restart, cancellation, retry and reprocessing must not duplicate structured records or embeddings. Changed-source revisions preserve historical citations and do not silently overwrite grade interpretations. Record adapter/version and validation status. Text readiness, structured readiness and collection coverage are separate visible states. Disable/restore and current access restrictions apply to both passage and structured evidence.
6. **Bounded linked-document discovery and capture.** Add an admin-defined collection scope and a previewable manifest of discovered indexes, sections, content pages and pagination links. Resolve relative links against the final document URL and distinguish navigation relationships from evidence content. Permit explicitly selected sibling book paths; do not assume that every child lives under the starting directory. Follow only allowed hosts/paths, with depth, document-count, byte and time limits, cycle detection, duplicate handling, conservative request rates and bounded retries. Reuse server-side safe fetching and its destination/redirect checks on every request; do not execute page scripts or bypass access restrictions. Separate fragment-free fetch identity from anchor locations: fetch a file once while retaining all referenced anchors, printed-page labels and parent/section relationships. Preserve immutable originals, checksums, encoding, requested/final URLs and fetch provenance using the shared asset/revision model. Show discovered, fetched, excluded, failed and unresolved targets, limit exhaustion and missing anchors; partial coverage must never be labeled complete. Resume/cancel/retry must preserve the manifest without duplicate assets or entries.
7. **Meaningful-content extraction and filtering.** Save substantive, source-faithful content as reviewable blocks and structured records; only approved meaningful content may enter chunks, embeddings and retrieval. Exclude advertisements, menus, repeated navigation, cookie banners, scripts, tracking markup and unrelated boilerplate from the evidence corpus. Index-only pages may supply discovery/hierarchy metadata without becoming evidence passages. Retain headings, rubric ancestry, remedy notation, grade-bearing typography, tables, footnotes, cross-references, negation and modalities; short entries and repeated domain text are not junk merely because they are short or repeated. Preserve verified continuation context across documents without combining unrelated sections or editions. Use layout-aware rules/adapters and explicit quality checks; unsupported or ambiguous extraction enters review rather than silently dropping content or inventing structure. Any model-assisted classification/mapping is a reviewable suggestion, not replacement source text. Retain the immutable original separately for audit and exact citations; “meaningful content only” applies to derived evidence and indexing, not destructive rewriting of source assets. Record exclusion reasons and adapter/filter versions, flag empty or suspiciously sparse results, and allow admins to inspect and restore incorrectly excluded substantive blocks through a new processing revision.
8. **Collection and content review.** Provide collection-level inclusion/exclusion and coverage review, then a side-by-side inert saved-original view and extracted-content view with exact supporting locations. Show proposed hierarchy, continuation links, excluded blocks/reasons, unresolved aliases/grades and extraction warnings. Admins can accept, correct or reject content and mappings before existing rights, publication and index gates apply. Display collection coverage, text readiness and structured readiness separately; successful fetching alone is not approval or verified structure. Saved citations must open the correct archived document/anchor without refetching the live site.

## Boundaries and following work

No full patient repertorization, automated remedy ranking/prescribing, diagnosis or exhaustive literature coverage is implied. No automatic clinical-evidence upgrade follows from category or grade. ING-06 now includes the reusable bounded linked-document discovery, meaningful-content filtering and admin review needed for this feature; this explicitly brings that collection foundation forward from ING-03. ING-03 expands collection coverage on the same foundation rather than introducing a parallel crawler or corpus; ING-04 adds schema-aware XML; ING-05 extends the same classification, structured-readiness, retrieval and citation contracts across those formats/collections. No unrestricted whole-site crawl or universal semantic adapter is implied. PROD-01 operational recovery remains deferred per the product priority.
